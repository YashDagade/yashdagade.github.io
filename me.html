Yash Dagade

Hey, I’m Yash.

I love building. I’m a junior at Duke. I am interested in energy and intelligence; I think they are the defining problems of our generation. I like to think deeply about the nature of intelligence and to study how we normatively ought to build it. This has inspired my love for energy-based functions and JEPAs. For the past year and a half or so, I have been fortunate to work with Haoran Zhu, Yilun Kuang and Yann LeCun. Our work probes the characteristics of optimal energy functions.

Intrinsically, I have always loved ambitious problems. I love pursuing dreams that keep me away from sleep. Ultimately, I think meaning in life comes from forming deep, meaningful relationships with the people around us. I have been fortunate to find a very fun way of doing this: working on big, ambitious problems with a small group of people who always seem to redefine what is possible.

I am quite interested in philosophy, especially Girard, Strauss and Nietzsche. I also love studying people in history who went on to pursue the most enormous, ambitious dreams they could dream of. I feel that, intrinsically, they were all living a very similar life: the life of a lover who was autotelically in love with their art. I hope to pursue this same love in my own way.

I have a deep love for nature. I find it heartbreaking that by the end of this century we could lose on the order of 10–30% of the species with which we currently share the planet. I believe we have the agency to solve our way out of these problems, but we need ambitious ideas. In high school, this inspired my work on SkyWindFarm, a more first-principles way of harnessing wind energy that is an order of magnitude more efficient than existing wind-based systems.

I am grateful for humanity, and for being alive right now. I am confident that, as a species, we will do well. This is the best time to be alive, and I am so grateful I get to partake in it.

I also love running, reading and going on walks.

Please do reach out if any of this resonates with you. I’d love to help :D
