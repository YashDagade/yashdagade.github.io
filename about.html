About

I love to build.

I'm interested in energy and intelligence. They seem to be the defining problems of our generation.

I'm currently doing research at CILVR on robotics and planning: world models that learn sparse, maximum-entropy representations and use them to plan further into the future.

[1]–[4] Things I have built
  • LpWM A JEPA world model with non-negative sparse representations; sparsity lowers the predictor capacity needed for planning (paper). [1] 2026
  • Rectified LpJEPA Controllable sparsity for self-supervised learning via maximum-entropy sparse representations. ICML 2026 (paper, blog). [2] 2026
  • Radial-VCReg VICReg plus a radial Gaussianization loss that moves a broader class of feature distributions toward normality. NeurIPS workshops (paper). [3] 2025
  • SkyWindFarm Harnessing high-altitude wind power at scale (explanation, flying video, utility patent). [4] 2022–24
More things I have built

  • Option Glass A macOS screen assistant: double-tap Option to ask about anything on screen, and the answer streams into a small glass panel. 2026
