Soundtracks

The Build page has had three soundtracks. Each button opens that version of the page in a new tab, so you can play them one after another and compare.

Click or tap once inside a tab to turn its sound on (browsers require it). [space] pauses the film, [S] mutes a tab. You can also press [1], [3] or [4] here.

  1. r1

    Round 1 — microsound + granular bed (had the flute)

    No composed music yet. The film has only its on-screen sounds, and the index settles into a shimmering cloud of glassy grains: the “flute”.

    Film
    Keystroke clicks as each line types, tuned ticks on photo flips and plots, glitch hits when a line slams in. No score: the music file was still a placeholder.
    Index
    Glassy sine grains from D5 to E6 (about 0.6–1.3 kHz), a low sub pulse every two bars and a few high ticks.
    Listen
    For the flute, use “Skip to index” [↵]: the grain cloud fades in about a second later.

  2. r3

    Round 3 — first composed score, 118 BPM

    A 44-bar minimal techno / IDM score in D major pentatonic, written to the film: it builds, drops, goes silent, builds again and ends on a soft chord.

    Film
    Clicky kick, micro hats, rim ticks, clean sub bass, a warm low pad and muted keys, with the typing clicks on top.
    Index
    Dark low grains (D3–D4), slow sub pulses and a few soft ticks. The flute is gone.
    Listen
    The first drop lands at 0:40 (bar 21); the film runs 1:29.

  3. r4

    Bits & Atoms current

    Tactile software and hardware sounds instead of a drum kit, starting almost silent and speeding up into an “assembly engine”.

    Bits
    Mechanical keyboard switches, terminal chimes, compile ticks, UI blips.
    Atoms
    Micro-servo whirs, pneumatic hiss, robotic-arm glides, metal snap-fits, relays, a machining hum.
    End
    A “compile success” chime as the film settles, then a crossfade into the calm background bed.

What changed

r1 → r3 · rounds 2 and 3

  • The flute went away. The sustained high “flute / whistle” tone was too much, so grains and tonal parts moved below about 900 Hz and bright layers were low-passed. The index bed dropped two octaves, from D5–E6 to D3–D4.
  • Real music arrived: the placeholder became a 44-bar score locked to the film (intro, build, riser, drop, a bar of silence, half-time, breakdown, final drop, outro).
  • Round 3 gave low notes a little extra body so they carry on laptop and phone speakers, turned the score down 5 dB and brought the typing clicks forward.

r3 → r4 · round 4

  • Micro-foley replaces the drum kit: keystrokes, relays and snap-fits play the rhythm, inspired by Paradigm’s Fourth Fund film and OpenAI’s Super Bowl spot.
  • A new shape: instead of techno build-ups and drops, it starts with single clicks in near silence and speeds up by density into one dense crescendo.
  • A clear ending: the compile-success chime, then a crossfade into the background bed.
  • Kept: no flute, the same pitch limits (round 2) and the small-speaker body (round 3).

All three play under the same film of the build poem.

r1 was rebuilt from the round-1 build logs; its sound engine matches, byte for byte, the backup made at the start of round 2. In the r1 and r3 archives, the scenes other than Build come from round 3.
